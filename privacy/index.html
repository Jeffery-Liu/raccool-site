Privacy Policy

RACCOOL Interactive Inc. · Effective October 17, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you visit raccool.ca and how RACCOOL Interactive Inc. ("we") handles it. The short version: this website has no accounts, no forms, no advertising and no cookies, and we do not sell personal information.

What we collect

  • Email you send us. If you write to contact@raccool.ca, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to reply to you and keep them only as long as needed for that conversation or our business records.
  • Visit statistics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It does not use cookies or track you across sites. We see only totals, such as how many people visited, which pages they viewed, which site referred them, their general device and browser type, and their country.

Services that load when you visit

Like most websites, this page is delivered with the help of other companies. Each of them receives your IP address and basic browser information as part of normal internet traffic:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and may log visits for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts provides the typefaces. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr provides the 3D library used for the card on the home page.
  • Cloudflare provides the visit statistics above and forwards email sent to our address. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Stored on your device

If you switch between the light and dark theme, your browser remembers that choice using local storage. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time through your browser settings.

Links to other sites

Links to Discord, Steam and other platforms take you to services we do not control. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

We are based in British Columbia, Canada. Under B.C.'s Personal Information Protection Act and Canada's PIPEDA, you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent. Email contact@raccool.ca and we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
